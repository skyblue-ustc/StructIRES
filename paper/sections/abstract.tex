\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Motivation:} IRES engineering is confounded by structural ensembles, assay provenance, and
downstream cargo context. Existing learning-based designs are dominated by a short DNA/lentiviral
reporter library whose transfer to direct-RNA measurements and full-length IRESes is uncertain.
\textbf{Results:} We develop an assay-labelled benchmark and generator-agnostic multi-objective
framework for conservative mutation of experimentally supported IRESes. It combines independent
function scorers, ensemble-structure preservation, cargo-crosstalk control, applicability, and
diversity under matched query budgets. In a reconstructed-fold shortcut audit, hashed 3--6-mer
features reached AUC 0.723 and AUPR 0.452. Cross-assay and constrained-design effect sizes remain
predeclared pending locked experiments; computational candidates are not claimed as experimentally
improved. \textbf{Availability and implementation:} Code and frozen manifests will be released
after patent/disclosure review. \textbf{Contact:} To be completed. \textbf{Supplementary
information:} Available with the submitted manuscript.
\end{abstract}
