\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Motivation:} Learning-based IRES design commonly optimizes a sequence score alone. For
full-length IRESes, this can select mutations that increase a model score while disrupting the
parent energy landscape and structural ensemble, and reporter-trained scores may not transfer to
direct-RNA settings. We address this objective-misalignment problem with constraint-guided IRES
optimization rather than treating a legacy classifier as a complete design oracle.
\textbf{Results:} We introduce \textsc{StructIRES}, a structure-aware IRES framework that couples
validation-clean recognition with constrained full-length candidate ranking. Starting from public
RNA-FM initialization, its recognition model pools contextual residue pairs over label-free
ViennaRNA MFE contacts through a gated residual. Across three matched native folds, this contact
fusion changes mean AUC/AUPR/F1 from 0.770/0.600/0.521 to 0.774/0.601/0.533 relative to an
otherwise identical sequence-only re-train; this legacy-fold signal does not establish strict
identity-aware generalization, where a profile-fusion ablation did not improve ranking.
Calibration also requires further improvement. For
full-length ranking, \textsc{StructIRES-Rank} combines the released IRES-LM ensemble with
parent-relative energy, global ensemble pairing, and high-confidence parent-anchor preservation.
Across three frozen 512-candidate mutation pools for each of ten experimentally supported seeds,
all five arms receive identical candidate sets and selection budgets. Relative to IRES-LM
score-only selection, the combined constraint set improves the held-out direct-RNA computational
proxy from 0.419 to 0.435 (paired gain 0.0158, 95\% bootstrap CI 0.0079--0.0242), reduces absolute
MFE deviation from 4.021 to 0.666 kcal/mol, lowers pairing-profile distance from 0.164 to 0.037,
and increases parent ensemble-anchor retention from 0.646 to 0.955. Our results establish
computational candidate enrichment and structural preservation, not experimentally verified
translation improvement.

\end{abstract}
