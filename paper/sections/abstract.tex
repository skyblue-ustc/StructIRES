\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Motivation:} IRES engineering is confounded by structural ensembles, assay provenance, and
downstream cargo context. Existing learning-based designs are dominated by a short DNA/lentiviral
reporter library whose transfer to direct-RNA measurements and full-length IRESes is uncertain.
\textbf{Results:} We develop an assay-labelled benchmark and generator-agnostic multi-objective
framework for conservative mutation of experimentally supported IRESes. It combines independent
function scorers, ensemble-structure preservation, cargo-crosstalk control, applicability, and
diversity under matched query budgets. In a reconstructed-fold shortcut audit, hashed 3--6-mer
features reached AUC 0.723 and AUPR 0.452, but fell to AUC 0.578 and AUPR 0.250 on an exact
90\%-identity cluster-disjoint 174-nt split. Zero-shot transfer to 2,179 labelled direct-RNA
sequences further fell to AUC 0.368 and AUPR 0.037. A composition control transferred at AUC 0.579
and AUPR 0.058. A full ten-checkpoint RNA-FM rerun reproduced its native benchmark
(AUC $0.778\pm0.009$, AUPR $0.614\pm0.014$), yet its predeclared direct-RNA ensemble reached
only AUC 0.600 and AUPR 0.058, with specificity 0.001 and ECE 0.906 at the released threshold.
Individual transfer AUCs ranged from 0.251 to 0.821 despite similar native performance.
Constrained-design effect sizes remain
predeclared pending matched-budget experiments; computational candidates are not claimed as
experimentally improved.

\end{abstract}
