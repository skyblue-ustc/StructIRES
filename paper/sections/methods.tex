\section{Methods}

\subsection{Data provenance and assay labels}
Every record retains its source study, assay context, construct, cell type, length, IRES type or
family, and evidence level. DNA/lentiviral reporter labels, circRNA-plasmid measurements, and
direct-RNA measurements are never pooled into an unlabeled target. Exact and similarity-based
overlaps are frozen before model fitting.

\subsection{IRES identification benchmark}
The released random-split file was audited as ten repeated stratified 90/10 holdouts rather than a
mutually exclusive ten-fold partition. Published values are retained as external references. For a
leakage-aware random-split pilot, we reconstruct deterministic folds stratified by label, source,
and coarse length group, and reserve the next fold for threshold selection. The strict view clusters
the dominant 174-nt subset by exhaustive all-pairs ungapped Hamming identity at 90\%, takes graph
connected components, and assigns whole components to a deterministic 70/15/15
train/validation/test split. All identity edges are checked after assignment and any crossing edge
invalidates the split. This exact equal-length view is reported separately from the pending gapped,
cross-length and family-held-out audits. AUPR is primary
because positives comprise about 19.6\% of the published dataset; AUC, F1, calibration, and
source/length-stratified performance are secondary outcomes. As a strict source-holdout control,
models are fitted only to the 44,634-sequence 55k library, with ten internal validation folds for
threshold selection and ensembling, and are evaluated once on all 276 \texttt{IRESite\_exp} records.
The held-out source is not used for fitting, thresholding, calibration, or model selection.
Released RNA-FM, UTR-LM and IRESfinder models are also executed on their author-native repeated
holdouts. Every local run stores predictions, input and checkpoint hashes, and a run manifest. We
report mean and sample standard deviation across holdouts but do not interpret them as independent
folds. The released RNA-FM and UTR-LM checkpoints were selected upstream on test AUPR, so these
rows are protocol reproductions rather than independent blind-test estimates.

\subsection{Cross-assay audit}
Models trained on the legacy reporter assay are evaluated without adaptation on normalized
IRES-TrAPPr measurements. We report rank association, calibration where labels permit, top-$k$
overlap, and disagreements stratified by length, type, source, and distance from the training
distribution. As low-capacity shortcut controls, composition logistic regression and hashed
3--6-mer linear classifiers are fitted as ten-member ensembles. Each member excludes one legacy
test fold and the following fold for validation; direct-RNA labels are never used for fitting,
threshold selection, or model choice. AUC and AUPR uncertainty is estimated with 2,000 stratified
bootstrap resamples of the direct-RNA evaluation set.
For RNA-FM, each released checkpoint is first evaluated separately. The predeclared transfer
result is the arithmetic mean probability across all ten checkpoints; no fold, probability
direction or threshold is selected using direct-RNA labels. Pairwise prediction correlations and
per-checkpoint direct-RNA metrics are reported only as a frozen post hoc stability audit.

\subsection{Seeded full-length optimization}
Starting from ten experimentally supported full-length viral IRESes, we deterministically generated
three shared pools (seeds 42, 43 and 44), each containing 512 unique substitution mutants per parent.
Each mutant retained length and made at most 3\% substitutions. Random, score-only, structure-only
and first-pass robust-full selection all retained 50 candidates from the identical parent-specific
pool. Score-only ranked frozen local RNA-LM teacher-forcing likelihood. Structure-only equally ranked
absolute MFE deviation and ensemble pairing-profile distance from the parent. First-pass robust-full
equally ranked RNA-LM likelihood, MFE preservation and ensemble preservation. Thus the first-pass
robust-full result tests a controlled structure--plausibility trade-off; cargo context, direct-RNA
calibration, applicability and diversity enter only in the next prespecified extension.

\subsection{Evaluation and statistics}
Optimization scorers are not used as sole final evaluators. For the first pass, we report the
parent-by-run means over 30 paired units and bootstrap confidence intervals for robust-full minus
score-only effects. RNA-LM likelihood is explicitly a proposal-plausibility score; the resulting
effects are interpreted as computational structural robustness, not activity, unless prospectively
validated in an appropriate assay.
