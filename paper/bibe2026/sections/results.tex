\section{Results}

\subsection{IRES recognition establishes the sequence baseline}
The first task is IRES identification: a sequence-level classifier estimates
whether an RNA is IRES-like under the historical reporter benchmark. The
released RNA-FM, UTR-LM, and IRESfinder implementations were locally rerun on
their author-native repeated holdouts (Table~\ref{tab:recognition}). These
results establish a reproducible sequence baseline for the proposal score used
in constrained design. Published values for IRES-LM, DeepCIP, and IRESpy are
retained as context but are visibly separated because their data and protocol
conditions are not interchangeable.

We next retrained the author-style RNA-FM classifier from the same public t12
prior while reserving a stratified subset of each source-training fold for
epoch and threshold selection. The matched \textsc{StructIRES-Classifier}
differs only by its label-free MFE-contact encoder and zero-initialized gated
residual. Across folds 0, 2 and 4, sequence-only re-training obtained
AUROC/AUPR/F1/MCC of
$0.770\pm0.005$/$0.600\pm0.018$/$0.521\pm0.016$/$0.426\pm0.012$;
contact fusion obtained
$0.774\pm0.007$/$0.601\pm0.022$/$0.533\pm0.024$/$0.439\pm0.047$.
The mean changes are therefore +0.004 AUROC, +0.002 AUPR, +0.013 F1 and
+0.013 MCC. ECE increases by 0.043, so the result is a controlled
legacy-reporter discrimination signal rather than a calibration claim.

The native holdouts are sequence-proximal and overlap across the released
repeated-fold protocol. We therefore also retained a separate 90\%
identity-component-disjoint test. In that harder three-seed evaluation,
sequence-only attained AUC/AUPR/F1
$0.559\pm0.004$/$0.188\pm0.005$/$0.295\pm0.002$, whereas a BPP/MFE
profile-fusion control attained
$0.557\pm0.017$/$0.183\pm0.003$/$0.297\pm0.010$. Thus the strict audit does
not establish general identity-aware ranking improvement and is not used to
select the native classifier or design model.

\subsection{Matched constraints preserve complementary properties}
Score-only selection yields high IRES-LM scores but substantial parent drift
(Table~\ref{tab:ablation}). Energy, global-ensemble, and anchor terms each
improve their intended structural quantity. The combined
\textsc{StructIRES-Rank} preserves all three simultaneously: it reduces mean
absolute MFE deviation by 83.4\%, pairing-profile distance by 77.4\%, and
increases parent-anchor retention by 0.309 relative to score-only selection.
All arms operate on the same 512 candidates per parent and retain the same 50
candidates, so this pattern cannot be attributed to a larger search budget.

\subsection{Direct-RNA mutational knowledge acts as a transparent guardrail}
The public IAPV scan contains 208 A-substitution tiles covering 169 of 213
parent positions. For the IAPV-only matched ablation, adding its edit-risk term
decreases mean MPRA edit risk from $2.438\pm0.174$ to $1.782\pm0.087$
(paired difference $-0.656$, bootstrap interval $-0.933$ to $-0.480$). This
comes with a transparent MFE-preservation trade-off (0.641 to 0.887 kcal/mol),
so it is kept separate from the ten-parent headline result.

\subsection{A matched structural case makes preservation explicit}
Figure~\ref{fig:structure-case} compares the deterministic rank-1 IAPV
candidates selected from frozen pool 42. The score-only candidate has a
slightly higher IRES-LM probability (0.976 versus 0.963), but $|\Delta
\mathrm{MFE}|=3.70$ kcal/mol and all-anchor retention of 0.682. In contrast,
the \textsc{StructIRES-Rank} candidate has zero MFE deviation and 1.000
all-anchor retention. To maintain print-scale readability, the visual overlay
uses the 16 highest-probability parent anchors; the numerical retention values
use all 64 parent anchors. This is a representative mechanism illustration,
not an activity measurement or a claim about experimentally mapped IRES motifs.

\subsection{Assay shift motivates constrained rather than score-only design}
Released RNA-FM and UTR-LM checkpoint reruns recover their native benchmark
summaries, confirming that the published classifiers are meaningful baseline
proposal models. Yet on direct-RNA labels, RNA-FM checkpoint AUROCs range from
0.251 to 0.821 and the all-checkpoint ensemble has AUROC 0.600 and AUPR 0.058
at 5.1\% active prevalence. The released IRESfinder model is below random
ranking (AUROC 0.460) in the same transfer setting. Hence, the structural
terms are not presented as a substitute activity assay; they are conservative
constraints against over-optimizing an assay-specific score.
