## Formatting contract

- Keep title, abstract, sections, tables, figures, and bibliography in separate files.
- Maintain vector PDF figures and readable single-column fallbacks.
- Keep the main method/result narrative within ten IEEE two-column pages as a conference stress
  test, but retain full analyses for the journal/supplementary package.
- Never put essential controls only in an appendix when a candidate venue disallows appendices.
- Report mean and standard deviation or confidence intervals over folds/seeds; never paste only the
  best run.
- Freeze terminology: computational enrichment/robustness without wet-lab verification.

### Bioinformatics Original Paper wrapper

- Target approximately 5,000 words and no more than seven journal-template pages, excluding
  figures; the journal warns that materially over-length papers may be returned before review.
- Convert the current provisional abstract to a maximum-150-word structured abstract with
  `Motivation`, `Results`, `Availability and Implementation`, `Contact`, and `Supplementary
  Information` headings when the result and repository-release status are frozen.
- Prepare the mandatory cover letter explaining fit to computational molecular biology.
- Keep the first submission format-free if that reduces conversion work; apply the current journal
  template at revision/acceptance rather than treating the internal article class as final format.
- Actual biological data are required; this project uses assay measurements and must not become a
  simulation-only design paper.
